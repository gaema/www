accelx

A GPU compute stack for Arm Mali — its own runtime, compiler and Vulkan driver — for running neural-network inference on Mali GPUs.

Available for license

What it is

accelx is a replacement for the vendor and Mesa compute paths on Arm Mali GPUs, written for inference. It submits work to the GPU through the standard Linux kernel driver, compiles kernels for the Mali instruction sets itself, and runs whole language models through the gaema inference engine with no Mesa library loaded.

It is aimed at SoC vendors, single-board-computer makers and device manufacturers who ship a Mali GPU and want on-device inference without depending on a closed vendor driver.
